TSBIE 2nd Year Chemistry — High-Yield 1:5 Question Bank

Target: 60/60 Marks | Complete Blueprint Coverage

SECTION A: Very Short Answer Questions (10 × 2 = 20 Marks)

All questions compulsory. No internal choice.

Q1: Solutions

  • What are isotonic solutions? Give an example.
  • Calculate the mole fraction of H₂SO₄ in a solution containing 98% H₂SO₄ by mass.
  • Define osmotic pressure.
  • State Henry's law.
  • What are colligative properties? Give examples.

Q2: Electrochemistry & Chemical Kinetics

  • What is metallic corrosion? Give one example.
  • Give the electrode reactions occurring at the anode and cathode in an H₂–O₂ fuel cell.
  • Define order of a reaction and illustrate with an example.
  • What are pseudo first-order reactions? Give one example.
  • A reaction has a half-life of 10 minutes. Calculate the rate constant for the first-order reaction.

Q3: General Principles of Metallurgy

  • What is the role of cryolite in the metallurgy of aluminium?
  • What is matte? Give its composition.
  • Give the composition of: (a) Brass (b) Bronze (c) German silver.
  • What is blister copper? Why is it so called?
  • Explain the terms gangue and slag.

Q4: p-Block Elements

  • Why is the nitrogen molecule highly stable?
  • Why is a mixture of He and O₂ used in modern diving apparatus?
  • What is tailing of mercury? How is it removed?
  • What happens when white phosphorus is heated with concentrated NaOH solution in an inert atmosphere of CO₂?
  • NH₃ forms hydrogen bonds but PH₃ does not—why?

Q5: d- & f-Block Elements & Coordination Compounds

  • What is an ambidentate ligand? Give an example.
  • Zn²⁺ is diamagnetic whereas Mn²⁺ is paramagnetic—why?
  • CuSO₄·5H₂O is blue in colour whereas anhydrous CuSO₄ is colourless—why?
  • Calculate the spin-only magnetic moment of Fe²⁺(aq) ion.
  • What is Misch-metal? Give its composition and uses.

Q6: Organic Chemistry

  • What are ambident nucleophiles?
  • Explain the reaction of Aniline with nitrous acid (Diazotization).
  • What are enantiomers?
  • Explain the Wurtz-Fittig and Wurtz reactions with one example each.
  • Write the equation for the carbylamine reaction of an aliphatic primary amine.

Q7 & Q8: Polymers

  • What is PHBV? How is it useful to man?
  • What do you mean by polydispersity index (PDI)?
  • What is a biodegradable polymer? Give two examples.
  • What is vulcanization of rubber?
  • Write the names and structures of the monomers of: (i) Buna-S (ii) Buna-N (iii) Dacron (iv) Neoprene.
  • What is Ziegler-Natta catalyst?
  • Mention the structures and names of the monomers used for: (a) Bakelite (b) Teflon (c) Nylon-6,6 (d) PVC (e) Polystyrene.
  • What is polymerization? Give an example.
  • What is an addition polymer? Give an example.
  • What are thermosetting polymers? Give an example.

Q9 & Q10: Everyday Life & Biomolecules

  • What are antacids? Give an example.
  • What are artificial sweetening agents? Give an example.
  • What are food preservatives? Give an example.
  • What are disinfectants? Give an example.
  • What are antihistamines? Give an example.
  • What is tincture of iodine and what is its use?
  • What are tranquilizers? Give an example.

  • What is a Zwitter ion? Give an example.

SECTION B: Short Answer Questions (6 × 4 = 24 Marks)

Answer any 6 out of 8 questions.

Q11: Solid State

  • Derive Bragg's equation.
  • What are Schottky and Frenkel defects?
  • Classify each as p-type or n-type semiconductor: (i) Ge doped with In (ii) Si doped with B.
  • What are F-centers and explain antiferromagnetism with a suitable example?
  • In terms of band theory, what is the difference between a conductor and a semiconductor?

Q12: Solutions

  • State Raoult's law. The vapour pressure of pure water at 293 K is 17.535 mm Hg. Calculate the vapour pressure of the solution at 293 K when 25 g of glucose is dissolved in 450 g of water.
  • What is relative lowering of vapour pressure (RLVP)? How is it useful to determine the molar mass of a non-volatile solute?
  • Calculate the mass of a non-volatile solute (molar mass 40 g/mol) to be dissolved in 114 g octane to reduce vapour pressure to 80%.
  • A solution of glucose in water is labeled as 10% w/w. What would be the molarity of the solution?
  • Define mole fraction and calculate the mole fraction of H₂SO₄ in a solution containing 98% H₂SO₄ by mass.

Q13: Surface Chemistry

  • What are the different types of adsorption? Give four differences between physisorption and chemisorption.
  • What is catalysis? How is catalysis classified? Give two examples for each.
  • What is an emulsion? Explain the classification of emulsions with examples.
  • What are micelles? Discuss the mechanism of micelle formation and cleansing action of soap.
  • How are colloids classified on the basis of interaction between dispersed phase and dispersion medium (Lyophilic vs. Lyophobic)?

Q14: Metallurgy

  • Explain the purification/concentration of sulphide ore by the froth flotation method.
  • Differentiate between roasting and calcination by giving suitable examples.
  • Outline the principles of refining metals by: (a) Zone refining (b) Electrolytic refining (c) Poling (d) Vapour phase refining.
  • Explain briefly the extraction of aluminium from bauxite.
  • Write down the chemical reactions taking place in the extraction of zinc from zinc blende.

Q15: d- & f-Block Elements & Coordination Compounds

  • Explain Werner's theory of coordination compounds with suitable examples.
  • Write the characteristic properties of transition elements.
  • Write the IUPAC systematic names for: (i) [Co(NH₃)₆]Cl₃ (ii) [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl(NH₂CH₃)]Cl (iii) [Ti(H₂O)₆]³⁺ (iv) K₂[PdCl₄] (v) K₃[Fe(CN)₆] (vi) [Ni(CO)₄].
  • Write the IUPAC formulae for: (i) Tetrahydroxozincate(II) (ii) Hexaamminecobalt(III) sulphate (iii) Potassium tetrachloropalladate(II) (iv) Potassium tri(oxalato)chromate(III).
  • Explain the terms: (i) Ligand (ii) Coordination number (iii) Coordination entity (iv) Central metal atom.

Q16: Biomolecules

  • Explain denaturation of proteins with suitable examples.
  • Give the sources of the following vitamins and name their deficiency diseases: (a) Vitamin A (b) Vitamin D (c) Vitamin E (d) Vitamin K.
  • What are hormones? Give one example for each: (i) Steroid hormones (ii) Polypeptide hormones (iii) Amino acid derivatives.
  • Discuss the structure of glucose on the basis of its chemical properties/reactions.
  • What are amino acids? Explain essential and non-essential amino acids with one example each.

Q17: Organic Chemistry

  • Explain SN1 and SN2 mechanisms with suitable examples.
  • Explain the acidic nature of phenols and compare it with that of alcohols.
  • Write the equations involved in the reaction of nitrous acid with ethylamine and aniline.
  • How will you carry out the conversions: (i) Ethane to bromoethane (ii) Toluene to benzyl alcohol?
  • Write the structural formulas of: (i) 1-Bromo-4-sec-butyl-2-methylbenzene (ii) 2-Chloro-1-phenylbutane (iii) 4-tert-butyl-3-iodoheptane (iv) p-Bromochlorobenzene.

Q18: p-Block Elements

  • How are XeF₂, XeF₄, and XeF₆ prepared? Give their molecular structures.
  • How are XeO₃ and XeOF₄ prepared? Give their molecular structures.
  • What are interhalogen compounds? How are they classified? Give examples.
  • How does SO₂ react with: (a) Na₂SO₃ (b) Cl₂ (c) Fe³⁺ (d) KMnO₄?
  • How is chlorine prepared by the electrolytic method?

SECTION C: Long Answer Questions (2 × 8 = 16 Marks)

Answer any 2 out of 3 questions.

Q19: Electrochemistry & Chemical Kinetics

  • (a) State and explain Kohlrausch's law of independent migration of ions. (b) Give its applications.
  • (a) What is electrolysis? State Faraday's laws of electrolysis. (b) A solution of CuSO₄ is electrolysed for 10 minutes with a current of 1.5 amperes; calculate the mass of copper deposited at cathode.
  • Give a detailed account of the collision theory of reaction rates of bimolecular gaseous reactions.
  • (a) What is molecularity of a reaction? How is it different from order of a reaction? (b) What is rate of reaction? Explain the effect of temperature and catalyst on rate of reaction.
  • (a) What are galvanic cells? Explain the working of a Daniel cell with a neat sketch. (b) What are primary and secondary batteries with examples?

Q20: p-Block Elements

  • How is ammonia manufactured by Haber's process? Explain reactions of ammonia with: (a) ZnSO₄(aq) (b) CuSO₄(aq) (c) AgCl(s).
  • How is nitric acid manufactured by Ostwald's process? How does it react with: (a) Copper (b) Zn (c) S₈ (d) P₄?
  • How is chlorine prepared in the laboratory or by Deacon's method? How does it react with: (a) Iron (b) Cold/dilute vs. Hot/concentrated NaOH (c) Acidified FeSO₄ (d) Excess NH₃ (e) Ca(OH)₂?
  • Write the important chemical reactions and conditions involved in the manufacture of sulphuric acid by the Contact process.
